ABOUT THE CHAMPIONSHIP

South India's Ultimate Esports Arena

Executing from September 18th to October 4th, 2026 at Fiza by Nexus Mall, Mangaluru

ARENA X is the region's premier esports tournament, running across two electrifying weeks from September 18 to October 4, 2026 at the iconic Fiza by Nexus Mall in Mangaluru. Jointly organized by Fiza by Nexus, GAMEBOT, and Nitte Institute of Professional Education.

Whether you are competing in tactical PC shooters, head-to-head console brawlers, or high-speed racing duels across 19 official titles, this is your stage to conquer the leaderboard, claim high-stakes cash prizes, and earn total bragging rights.
